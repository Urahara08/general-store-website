About us

General Store sits at 30–31 The Square, right in the heart of Winchester. We're a small, friendly café serving proper coffee, hot chocolates, cakes, toasties and jacket potatoes by day, and wine, cocktails and charcuterie boards by night.

Grab a table under the awning, catch up with friends inside, or join us for one of our evenings of live music.
